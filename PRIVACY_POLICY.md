# Privacy Policy for InTime

**Last Updated:** May 26, 2026

## Overview

InTime ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

## Key Principle: Your Data Stays On Your Device

InTime is a **fully offline application**. All data is stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your information on remote servers.

## What Data We Collect

**We collect ZERO data.**

The App does not:
- Collect task data from your device
- Send any information over the network
- Track your usage patterns
- Use analytics or telemetry
- Identify you through any means

## How Data Is Stored

All your task information is stored in a local SQLite database on your device:
- Location: Your device's internal app storage
- Encrypted by: Your device's file system encryption (if enabled)
- Accessible by: Only this App and you (the user)

## Permissions Used

The App requests the following permissions (all used locally):

| Permission | Purpose | Network? |
|-----------|---------|----------|
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Send reminder notifications | No |
| `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Restore reminders after device restart | No |
| `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` | Precise reminder scheduling | No |

**None of these permissions involve network access or data transmission.**

## No Third-Party Services

The App does not:
- Use cloud services
- Integrate with third-party analytics
- Use advertising SDKs
- Depend on external servers
- Connect to the internet

## No Accounts Required

You do not need to:
- Create an account
- Log in
- Provide an email address
- Provide any personal information

## Export & Backup

You can export your tasks as a JSON file stored locally on your device. This backup file:
- Is generated by the App on your request
- Remains under your complete control
- Can be imported back into the App or shared manually
- Is never transmitted anywhere automatically

## Data Security

Your data is protected by:
1. **Device-level security** — Your device's operating system
2. **File permissions** — Only this App can access the database
3. **Local storage** — No transmission over networks

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted here with an updated date.

## Questions?

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub or contact the developer.
